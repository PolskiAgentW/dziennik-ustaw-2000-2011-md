> Protocol concerning amendment of the Agreement between the Government of the Reublic of Poland and the United Nations Human Settlements Programme on Establishment of a UN-Habitat Office in the Republic of Poland, signed in Nairobi on 7" April. 2005

> The Government of the Republic of Poland (hereinafter referred to as “the Government”) and the United Nations Human Settlements Programme (hereinafter referred to as “UN-Habitat”),

> Have agreed as follows:

> Article 1

> The date of termination of the Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Poland and the United Nations Human Settlements Programme on Establishment of a UN-Habitat Office in the Republic of Poland, hereinafter referred to as "the Agreement”, provided for by the article 10 paragraph 1 and article 16 shall be fixed on 31 December 2010.

> Article 2

> The financial contribution of the Government as well as method and conditions for the Government to finance the Office provided for by the article 10 paragraph 1 and article 11 of the Agreement remain unchanged.

> Article 3

> In paragraph 3 of the Annex to the Agreement should read as follows: “The personnel! of the Office shall be composed of Director and not more than five staff members.”

> Article 4

> This Protocol shall enter into force on the day of its signature. a Done at Warsaw on \t ae Ween 2008, in duplicate, each in the Polish and English languages, both texts being equally authentic.

> On behalf of the Government On behalf of the United Nations of the Republic of Poland Human Settlements Programm
